Acme Corporation

Autonomous yard operations for the modern distribution center.

The story
Series A · June 2026
The job

The building is automated. The yard is still a radio and a whiteboard.

Inside

A WMS knows every tote. Outbound waves are scheduled to the minute.

Outside

A lead spotter works a radio. Trailers wait. The night shift turns over.

The tax

Trailer dwell is the hidden cost on every wave. The analog yard is now the constraint.

Why now

The geometry repeats. Learned autonomy holds here first.

A public street is open world. A yard is painted lines and the same fifty trailers.

Closed world

Fences, docks, and a night shift. Snow and unmarked construction still fall back to a human.

Software first

Most buyers will not replace a working spotter truck. We attach to the fleet they already own.

Buyer is ready

VP of transportation owns dwell. The DC general manager owns the night shift. Both will take a paid pilot.

Product

Yard OS first. Vehicles optional.

01
